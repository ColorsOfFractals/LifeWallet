# Life Wallet

> **A home for the pieces of life.**

Life Wallet is a playful local-first desktop home for the scattered pieces of everyday life — the things worth keeping somewhere without turning life into another sterile dashboard.

  <img src="https://github.com/ColorsOfFractals/LifeWallet/releases/download/v0.1.1/Subscriptions.png"
       alt="Life Wallet subscriptions room"
       width="900">
</p>

## 🏠 Little rooms for ordinary life

Subscriptions, groceries, appointments, friend schedules, tasks, internet, and the wonderfully vague question:

**What am I forgetting?**

Life Wallet gives each piece its own little place, with tactile controls and deliberately playful machinery.
